Colombia Population

The tools for the collection of information in Colombia are in the hands of the National Administrative Department of Statistics -DANE-, an entity that was created in 1951 by Decree 2240, initially as the National Directorate of Statistics and by 1953 and under Decree 2263 acquires the name that is maintained to this day. Thus, the main function of DANE is “To produce and disseminate official statistical information, as a public good, with high standards of quality and technical rigor for decision making at national and territorial level, which contribute to the consolidation of a State with social, economic and environmental justice”. (Source: DANE). This project seeks to make an interpretation of the census information of Colombia during the last two decades of information available for consultation, in order to understand the social mobilization within the Colombian territory.
